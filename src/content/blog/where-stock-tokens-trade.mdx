Robinhood's developer documentation says that tokenized stocks "trade via RFQ at launch." That is true, and it is also not where the tokens are.

We read the chain directly on 21 July 2026, and found that **94 of the 96 stock tokens hold a non-zero balance in the Uniswap v4 `PoolManager`**. Not a handful of blue chips. Effectively the entire registry.

## The measurement

The Uniswap v4 architecture makes this easy to check, because every pool's tokens sit in one singleton contract: `PoolManager` at `0x8366a39CC670B4001A1121B8F6A443A643e40951`, verified on Robinhood Chain. So "how much of this stock token lives in Uniswap" is a single ERC-20 balance read.

We took the 96 active assets from Robinhood's public asset registry and called `balanceOf(PoolManager)` and `totalSupply()` on each. Both are exact onchain reads — no indexer, no estimate, no API in between. Anyone can re-run it against `https://rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com`.

| Token | In Uniswap v4 | Total supply | Share of supply |
|---|---|---|---|
| NU | 17.27 | 19.96 | **86.5%** |
| NFLX | 395.38 | 491.94 | **80.4%** |
| RIVN | 13.60 | 18.41 | **73.9%** |
| NVDA | 6,267.50 | 13,963.61 | **44.9%** |
| F | 21.81 | 49.34 | **44.2%** |
| SPCX | 3,408.82 | 8,313.42 | **41.0%** |
| MSFT | 361.85 | 1,732.82 | 20.9% |
| TSLA | 520.57 | 3,004.27 | 17.3% |
| AAPL | 373.56 | 2,625.54 | 14.2% |

Nearly half of every NVDA token in existence sits in one contract. The same contract holds roughly $8.3M of USDG on the other side of those pools.

## Why this matters more than it sounds

RFQ is a fine mechanism — a market maker quotes you a price, you take it or you don't. But RFQ liquidity is not composable. You cannot route through it, you cannot borrow against a quote, and another protocol cannot build on top of it without an integration.

An AMM pool is a public object. Its state is readable by any contract, its price is derivable by anyone, and its depth is a number rather than a relationship. The fact that stock token liquidity has landed overwhelmingly in Uniswap pools rather than staying in RFQ is the difference between tokenized equities being *tradeable* and being *composable*. It is why an onchain index protocol is possible on this chain at all.

It also means the honest answer to "where do I buy a stock token?" is not the one in the docs.

## The shape of the market

The rest of the picture is less flattering, and worth stating plainly.

**Onchain market capitalisation across all 96 tokens is roughly $17M.** That is the entire tokenized float of every Robinhood stock token combined — less than a rounding error against the companies they track. Tokenized equities are, at this moment, very early.

**24h volume is about $6.7M, and it is brutally concentrated.** NVDA alone accounts for around 60% of it. The top three tokens — NVDA, TSLA, SPCX — are roughly 86% of all trading.

Holder counts tell the same story from another angle. These are indexed from Transfer events by [Blockscout](https://robinhoodchain.blockscout.com):

Fifteen holders. The long tail is not thin, it is close to empty.

Supply leadership is its own surprise: the largest token supply is not NVDA but **USAR at 25,195 tokens**, ahead of NVDA's 13,964, SLV's 8,860 and SPCX's 8,313.

## Practical consequences

If you are buying, depth is the number that matters, not price. On the concentrated names you will trade close to the quote. On a token with 42 holders, the price you see and the price you get are different numbers, and the gap is the whole cost of the trade.

If you are building, the composability is real and available today, and the depth is not. Design for thin pools.

## A note on the numbers

Supply figures and Uniswap balances above are exact onchain reads we performed ourselves. Holder counts, 24h volume and the aggregate market capitalisation are explorer-reported and **indicative only** — we have since established that explorer prices for these tokens can be materially wrong, which is [its own article](/blog/verifying-price-sources). The order of magnitude holds; treat individual dollar figures as approximate.
